Gear Guide

Gear rarity stats, set bonuses, and unique weapon bonuses.

How Gear Works

  • Every set piece uses the same stat formula for its slot and rarity. Two set pieces in the same slot and rarity have identical base stats.
  • Weapons use the shared weapon formula. Their attack applies to their damage school: Melee, Ranged, or Magical.
  • Set bonuses are not cumulative. At 8 pieces, only the 8-piece tier is active.
  • Unique weapons are not part of a set. They receive normal weapon base stats plus their listed unique bonus.

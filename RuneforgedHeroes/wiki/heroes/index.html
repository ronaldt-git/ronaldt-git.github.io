Hero Roster

Base HP and Attack are Level 1 values before leveling, gear, promotions, Ascension, Power Runes, shrines, and skill-tree modifiers.

Promotion Bonuses

  • Roster bonus: Applies to every party hero. Contributions from owned heroes stack.
  • Self bonus: Applies only to the listed hero from 2 stars onward.
  • Values are cumulative at each star rank. Starting Shielded and Blessed stacks are granted at the start of a level or event.

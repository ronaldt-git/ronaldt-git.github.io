Guild Raid Boss

What makes it a Guild Raid

Guild Raid is a cooperative, shared-health boss fight for one guild. All damage from the guild contributes to the same boss during the active week.

  • A guild needs at least 5 members before its Guild Raid becomes available.
  • Your guild's selected raid boss and level determine the encounter.
  • Guild Raid boss levels run from 1 to 10.
  • When the guild slays the active boss, it earns a clear reward. The guild's Raid governance then determines the next boss level for the current week.
  • Your personal contribution ranks you only against members of your own guild.

Schedule and tickets

  • Guild Raids are active from Tuesday 3:00 AM Eastern until Monday 3:00 AM Eastern.
  • Monday 3:00 AM through Tuesday 3:00 AM is the weekly cooldown.
  • You receive 1 free Guild Raid Ticket each day.
  • Tickets are topped up to at least 1 at reset; extra tickets are never removed merely because a new day starts.
  • At zero tickets, you can watch 1 rewarded ad per day for 1 extra Guild Raid Ticket.
  • Guild Raid does not offer Gem ticket purchases.

Guild Raid rewards

Guild Raid has three reward lanes. They are separate and can all be earned.

  1. Battle rewards: Every completed Guild Raid fight grants Essence and Mythral based on damage dealt and boss level. These are not Global Raid rewards.
  2. Victory rewards: When the guild slays a boss, every guild member included in that clear receives Gems, Essence, and Mythral. The victory reward equals 10% of that level's first-place weekly reward, rounded up to the reward increments.
  3. Weekly participation rewards: At the weekly reset, contributors receive a Mail reward based on their personal damage rank inside the guild.

Runeforged Heroes

PvP Arena

Fights

  • Spend 1 Arena Ticket to challenge one of five offered saved teams.
  • Your team acts first. Defeat every enemy hero to win.
  • A completed PvP battle grants 10,000 Gold, whether the fight was won or lost.
  • Refreshing the five offered opponents costs 10,000 Gold.
  • Winning or losing changes Battle Points (BP), not Trophies directly.

Battle Points

Every new weekly season starts each entrant at 1,000 BP. BP determines only your placement inside the current bracket.

The five offers use these approved BP changes, from the strongest/highest-value offer to the weakest/lowest-value offer:

Offer value BP on win BP on loss
Highest +18 -6
High +13 -8
Even +10 -10
Low +8 -12
Lowest +6 -15

When you win, the real opponent loses half of your BP gain. When you lose, the real opponent gains half of your BP loss. NPC entries do not receive that update.

Tickets

  • You receive 3 free Arena Tickets each day.
  • Tickets are topped up to at least 3 at the daily reset; saved, purchased, or ad-earned tickets above 3 are not removed.
  • At zero tickets, you can earn up to 2 extra tickets per day from rewarded ads.
  • Arena Tickets cost 100 Gems each when purchased.

Leagues, brackets, and Trophies

Trophies determine your league. BP determines your rank inside that league's current weekly bracket.

League Trophy range Bracket size
Bronze 0–999 50
Silver 1,000–1,999 50
Gold 2,000–3,499 50
Crystal 3,500–4,999 40
Citrine 5,000–6,999 40
Diamond 7,000–8,999 40
Emerald 9,000–11,499 30
Ruby 11,500–13,999 30
Sapphire 14,000+ 25

Empty positions are filled by NPC teams. Real players never replace real players in a bracket.

At season settlement, final bracket rank changes Trophies. The top five positions award +1,500, +1,000, +750, +500, and +300 Trophies respectively; ranks 6–10 award +250. Lower ranks scale down through the middle of the bracket and become negative near the bottom. The game shows the projected Trophy change while the season is active.

If you miss one or more whole seasons without entering a bracket, the game applies -300 Trophies per missed season in one consolidated result when you return. No placement rewards are created for a season you never entered. This inactivity loss stops at the floor of the relevant league; players who were Emerald or higher cannot be taken below 9,000 Trophies by it.

Reset schedule and rewards

  • Daily placement rewards are sent to Mail at 3:00 AM Eastern.
  • Seasons end and the next season begins every Sunday at 3:00 AM Eastern.
  • Daily rewards contain Gold, Essence, and Gems. They are calculated from the same current-rank row as season rewards: one fifth of that row's Gold and Gems (Gems rounded to the nearest 5), with Essence equal to the daily Gold amount.
  • Season-result rewards contain Gems, Gold, and either Universal Hero Shards or Mystery Hero Shards. They are granted once at settlement and then presented in the season-result popup.

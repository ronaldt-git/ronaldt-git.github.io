Global Raid Boss

What makes it a Global Raid

Global Raid is not a shared-health boss. Every player fights the active weekly Level 8 boss against a fresh combat-health pool, then their damage is added to three worldwide reward lanes:

  1. Damage Milestones — personal cumulative weekly damage.
  2. Global Leaderboard — personal worldwide damage rank.
  3. Guild Leaderboard — worldwide rank of the total damage contributed for each guild.

These lanes stack. A player can earn milestone rewards, an individual leaderboard reward, and a guild leaderboard reward from the same weekly Global Raid.

The active boss rotates through Scorchfang, Voltjaw, Spider Queen, and Blightroot, the Corrupted One. All Global Raid bosses are always Level 8.

Schedule, combat health, and tickets

  • Global Raid is active from Tuesday 3:00 AM Eastern through Monday 3:00 AM Eastern.
  • Monday 3:00 AM through Tuesday 3:00 AM is the boss cooldown and settlement period.
  • Each Global Raid fight starts against a fresh 2.1 billion HP boss pool. It is not reduced by another player's fight.
  • At most 2.1 billion damage from one fight can count toward Global Raid progress and leaderboards.
  • You receive 1 free Global Raid Ticket each day. Tickets are topped up to at least 1, never reduced back down to 1.
  • At zero tickets, you can watch 1 rewarded ad per day for 1 extra ticket.
  • Additional Global Raid Tickets cost 200 Gems each, up to 10 per purchase. Ticket purchases remain available during cooldown so tickets can be prepared for the next boss.

Damage milestones

Your Global Raid damage accumulates for the full active week. Each threshold is a one-time, cumulative reward: earning 5 million damage also earns every earlier milestone.

Milestone rewards are granted and presented when you return to the Main Menu. Gear entries are resolved to the actual awarded gear before presentation.

Small-population personal Power Rune rule

If fewer than 100 players recorded Global Raid damage that week, the placement table still pays the same non-Rune rewards. Its Power Rune rewards are changed as follows:

  • 1st: 2 Level 2 Power Runes.
  • 2nd–3rd: 1 Level 2 Power Rune each.
  • Every other placement row that normally contains a Rune: same quantity, but Level 1.
  • Damage Milestone rewards do not use this population adjustment. In particular, the 10B milestone remains a Level 2 Power Rune.

Guild Leaderboard: total guild damage

Your Global Raid damage is also recorded for the guild you belonged to when that damage was dealt. If you leave the guild midweek, the damage you already contributed remains in that guild's weekly total.

Guild placement rewards are sent by Mail after weekly settlement to the qualifying guild recipients. They are independent of personal placement rewards.

Raid combat notes

Both raid modes use the raid-boss combat rules:

  • Raid bosses gain Enraged stacks as turns pass, becoming more dangerous in long fights.
  • Some boss attacks deal Void damage, which ignores damage reduction.
  • Raid bosses can only be stunned briefly before gaining temporary stun resistance.

The specific boss kit, targeting rules, and status effects vary by boss. Refer to the in-game boss information and combat tooltips for the current encounter details.

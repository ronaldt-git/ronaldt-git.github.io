Mechanics

Power Runes

Each hero equips one Power Rune. It increases only that hero's completed Attack and HP totals; it does not add secondary stats such as Crit, Damage Reduction, or Mana Generation.

The multiplier is applied after level, gear, set, and ordinary hero-modifier calculations, improving base stats and relevant flat and percentage bonuses already applied to Attack and HP.

Hero Leveling

Hero growth scales from each hero's Level 1 base Attack and HP, so heroes with stronger base stats retain proportionally stronger growth. Heroes earn one Skill Point every five completed levels.

  • HP growth above Level 1: (0.09 x L^2 + 24.3 x L + 120) - 144.39, multiplied by (base HP / 95)^0.85.
  • Attack growth above Level 1: (0.034 x L^2 + 8.3 x L + 10) - 18.334, multiplied by (base Attack / 10)^0.85.

Promotion Stars

A hero must reach its current level cap before promotion. Five stars is the maximum rank and permits Level 250.

Self bonuses apply only to that hero from 2 stars onward. Roster bonuses apply to every hero in the party from 1 star onward, and contributions from owned heroes stack.

Ascension

At 5 stars and Level 250, a hero can ascend from Rank 1 through Rank 10. Each rank costs one Ascension Core and 10 x target rank Universal Hero Shards, and grants one Skill Point. Ascension improves only level-derived base Attack and HP before gear, Power Runes, and ordinary modifiers.

Ascension Cores are exclusive to the Global Raid Boss 1B damage milestone. A player can receive only one Ascension Core from that milestone each week.

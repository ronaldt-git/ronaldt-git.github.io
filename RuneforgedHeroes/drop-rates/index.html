Exact Individual Gear-Piece Rates

The campaign pool contains 80 pieces across eight equipment slots. The game first chooses a slot uniformly, then chooses a piece uniformly within that slot; it does not choose uniformly from all 80 pieces.

Slot groupPieces in campaign poolChance for one named piece on one gear-drop roll
Weapon31 (21 set weapons plus 10 unique weapons)1 / (8 x 31) = 0.4032258%
Belt, boots, chestplate, elixir, helmet, necklace, or ring7 per slot1 / (8 x 7) = 1.7857143%

For a particular piece at a particular rarity, multiply the piece chance by that chapter's rarity chance. For example, a named non-weapon piece at Rare in Chapter 1 is 1/56 x 2% = 0.0357143% per gear-drop roll. When a level awards more than one gear item, each roll is independent.

Mission Random Gear Rewards

Random gear rewards use the shown rarity from their reward definition, then roll a piece from the configured pool.

  • For mission rewards like random common, uncommon, rare, or epic gear, rarity is fixed by the reward definition.
  • The specific piece is selected uniformly from the authoritative full gear-piece list for that reward type.
